Privacy Policy

Hex Island Generator (the "App")
Effective date: 6 October 2026

Hex Island Generator is an unofficial fan app that generates random hex-tile board layouts. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Catan GmbH or Catan Studio. CATAN is a trademark of its respective owner.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and the App has no user accounts. The board options you choose (for example which numbers and resources may touch) are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

Advertising

The App shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use data from your device, such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Ads may be personalized depending on your choices.

  • iOS: the App may ask for your permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the App shows Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice from the App's privacy options when it is available.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Settings → Google → Ads.

Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data about you. Data processed by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies, and you can use the controls described above. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored settings.
